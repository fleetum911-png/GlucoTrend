GlucoTrend

A continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) machine learning project built on the GlucoBench benchmark dataset - covering target variable design, preprocessing, forecasting, and glucose-range classification.

Candidate target variables

  • Regression: glucose_lead_1 / 3 / 6 — forecast the next CGM reading(s)
  • Binary: target_binary — In Range vs Out of Range (70–180 mg/dL)
  • 3-class (ADA): target_ada3 — Hypoglycemia / Target Range / Hyperglycemia
  • 5-class (clinical tiers): target_5class — ATTD/ADA consensus severity levels
  • Trend: target_trend — quantile-calibrated rate-of-change direction

Dataset

Raw CGM readings, insulin, meals, activity, and biosignals across 10 patients.

Preprocessing

Missing-value handling, categorical encoding, scaling — mirrors the src/ pipeline.

Visualization

Correlation heatmaps and feature-vs-target boxplots from outputs/.

Models

Forecasting and classification models trained on the engineered targets.
