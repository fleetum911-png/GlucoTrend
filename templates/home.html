The modeling work

Three ways to learn
from the same signal.

Each classifier offers a different balance of clarity and flexibility. Their saved results are available on the Models page, alongside precision, recall, and F1.

01 INTERPRETABLE

Decision Tree

A sequence of feature-based splits that can be followed from input to output. A useful baseline when tracing model decisions matters.

Inspect the tree →
02 ENSEMBLE

Random Forest

Combines many trees trained on varied samples and feature subsets. Aggregation can make predictions less sensitive to one tree.

Explore the ensemble →
03 SEQUENTIAL

AdaBoost

Builds a sequence of small trees, increasing focus on observations earlier rounds classified poorly.
