  • Binary: target_binary — In Range vs Out of Range (70–180 mg/dL) — used by the live predictor
  • 3-class (ADA): target_ada3 — Hypoglycemia / Target Range / Hyperglycemia
  • 5-class (clinical tiers): target_5class — ATTD/ADA consensus severity levels
  • Trend: target_trend — quantile-calibrated rate-of-change direction
